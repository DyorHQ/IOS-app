# DyorHQ

The RWA HQ for social trading

DyorHQ is a self-custodial mobile app on Monad for launching stock-backed memecoins, copying on-chain traders, and trading perps and swaps.

## Identity concept

The signal D combines an open research lens with an outward-facing signal. It represents independent research, shared conviction, and action. Keep the symbol simple and flat; place glass, light, and depth in the surrounding interface.

Write the name as **DyorHQ**. Keep HQ together and on the same baseline. The wordmark uses Geist Semibold for Dyor and Geist Regular for HQ, with tight optical spacing.

## Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal | #B9F26B | Primary identity and important actions |
| Ink | #0C100D | Main dark background |
| Graphite | #1B211D | Cards and elevated surfaces |
| Paper | #F2F5EE | Text and light backgrounds |
| Positive | #27DB91 | Positive returns and buy direction |
| Negative | #FF507A | Negative returns and sell direction |

Use Ink and Paper for most of the interface. Signal should guide attention. Positive and Negative are semantic data colors, not alternative logo colors.

## Typography

- Geist Medium and Semibold: product headings and wordmark.
- Geist Regular: UI text and explanations.
- Geist Mono: addresses, order IDs, and precise technical data.
- Prefer sentence case. Keep body text at 16px and frequent labels at 14px where space allows.

## Logo use

- Use the chartreuse mark on Ink or Graphite.
- Use a solid Ink rendering on light backgrounds.
- Reserve at least one-quarter of the symbol's width as clear space.
- Use the symbol alone for app icons, avatars, and compact navigation.
- Use the wordmark for introductions, primary navigation, and brand communication.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add outlines, or place trading arrows inside the mark.
- Avoid applying gradients or glass distortion to the master symbol.

The supplied master is a transparent PNG. The website displays an Ink variant using a flat color treatment. This package does not include a manually constructed vector master.

## Voice

Clear, calm, informed, and socially aware. Give users the information needed to decide. Use real product terms: self-custodial wallet, stock-paired token, copy trade, swap, and perps.

The headline/tagline is exactly: **The RWA HQ for social trading**.

For token detail labels, distinguish a memecoin's pairing asset from direct stock ownership. Use “paired with aNVDA” when that describes the actual mechanism.

## Reference research

Iconly (https://iconly.design/) was inspected for small-size silhouette, spacing, one-color legibility, and app-icon recognition. Public, Acorns, Phantom, Craft, and Nike informed those general principles. DyorHQ's mark is an original design, not a reproduction of a reference logo.
